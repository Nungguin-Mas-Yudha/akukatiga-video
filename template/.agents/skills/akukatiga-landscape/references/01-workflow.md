# Stages and gates

Seven stages. Each ends with one line in chat saying what comes next, in plain words (`.agents/skills/stages/SKILL.md`).

| # | Stage | In, out | Gate | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research + outline | the approved topic -> `sources.md` (one row per claim: the claim, two sources, the verdict, and the **excerpt** that proves it, at most 3 sentences with the paper's section; papers fetched by Haiku through `node scripts/journal.mjs` into `research/`) -> `treatment.md`: 3 titles, the hook, 6-10 chapters with their beats, the retention question at each 2-3 min, the thread object per chapter, the science blocks with the question each answers and its one analogy | every claim has two reliable sources or is marked unverified and kept out; no Nobel-site text copied; **the creator approves the outline in words** | `references/02-script-and-research.md`, `references/08-storytelling.md`, `references/11-journal-sources.md` |
| 2 | Script | outline -> one `script.txt` per chapter, 3,000-3,800 words in all at 150-160 words a minute | `node scripts/script-check.mjs <film>` exits 0; then one reviewer (Science Editor, Newcomer, Story Editor, Listener) and one Fact Checker, both working from the excerpts in `sources.md`; output only BLOCK and FIX lines; round 2 only on changed lines and changed claims; then the creator reads it | `references/09-script-recipe.md`, `references/10-script-qc.md` |
| 3 | Voice + caption | `node film.mjs audition` -> the creator picks; `node film.mjs voice` (one whole take per chapter); `caption.txt` -> `node film.mjs align` -> `words.json`. Mechanical: Haiku runs it | the creator chose the voice; a take is regenerated whole, never spliced; every "!" line from align resolved; `node film.mjs status` shows the length >= minMinutes | `references/02-script-and-research.md`, `full-visual/references/05-workspace.md` |
| 4 | Visual plan (one page per chapter) | `direction.md` (one page: visual thesis, colour script, visual vocabulary, motifs, camera by act) and `plan.md`: **one line per scene**: what is on screen, its trigger word, its source picture or paper figure, its sound mode, and "2.5D: how" (which fake-3D technique). Three concepts only for the 1-2 hardest mechanisms of the film. Reference boards are gathered **after** approval, per scene, as the builder needs them | **the creator approves the direction and the plan in words** before any board, styleframe or code. A scene that cannot be done in 2.5D is changed here, not in the build. Every picture has its `references.md` row | `references/13-direction.md`, `references/03-pace-and-structure.md`, `references/05-footage-and-fair-use.md` |
| 5 | The hardest passage | the hardest 60 s built first as a draft; it is also the film's styleframe (no separate storyboard round) | machine scan; the creator watches with sound; one reviewer, FAIL table only. Nothing else is built until the creator accepts it | `references/04-look-and-frame.md`, `references/13-direction.md` section 5 |
| 6 | Chapters | chapter by chapter, one builder at a time; each chapter a draft with sound; the sound room places music and effects (`sound/spotting.md`, `node scripts/audio-check.mjs`) | per chapter: `node scripts/qc-scan.mjs <film> <chapter>` (Haiku runs it; pops, black, freeze, loudness, contact sheets; exits 1 on a pop), the creator's watch, one reviewer (FAIL table only), **at most 2 rounds**, round 2 on changed scenes only; remaining faults go in the delivery note | same, and `references/07-sound.md`, `references/14-sound-room.md` |
| 7 | Final + package | `node render.mjs <film> all` -> `node deliver.mjs <film>`; thumbnails (3 concepts), `description.md`, 3 short clips marked by timecode | `scripts/check.mjs` exits 0 (1440p, 24 fps, length, encode, loudness, true peak, no 6 s freeze, no black hole, subtitles, chapters); every credit the licence asks for is in the description; no hashtags or em dashes | `references/06-delivery.md`, `references/12-thumbnails.md` |
